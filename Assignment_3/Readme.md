This assignment compares the performance of three Large Language Models across three different AI problem categories: **Rider Allocation**, **Container Loading**, and **Kabaddi Game Strategy**.
Each problem contains **four difficulty levels**, and the models are evaluated on **accuracy, reasoning depth, mathematical reliability, and strategic insight**.

---

## **1. Rider Allocation Problem**

Focuses on assigning riders to orders under constraints such as distance, time delays, fairness, and policy rules.

### **Summary of Findings**

* **GPT-4**: Most reliable with strong calculations and consistent reasoning.
* **Claude**: Best for strategic, business-like explanations; sometimes makes severe math errors.
* **Gemini**: Clear and structured; good for clean explanations.

### **Overall Observation**

* **Simple tasks:** All models perform well.
* **Moderate tasks:** Claude excels in strategic framing.
* **Difficult tasks:** GPT-4 gives the most balanced and correct decisions.
* **Very difficult tasks:** Claude makes critical miscalculations; GPT-4 and Gemini remain reliable.

---

## **2. Container Loading Problem**

This involves 3D bin-packing, volume optimization, geometric fitting, and constraint satisfaction.

### **Summary of Findings**

* **Easy case:** GPT-4 & Gemini correct; Claude made multiplication error.
* **Medium case:** Gemini showed the best packing logic and explanation quality.
* **Hard case:** GPT-4 provided the most practical, transport-ready loading plan.

### **Overall Observation**

* **Best real-world planning:** GPT-4
* **Best conceptual teaching:** Gemini
* **Least reliable for math:** Claude

---

## **3. Kabaddi Game Strategy (AI Agents: Random, Greedy, Alpha-Beta, MCTS)**

Simulated game scenarios at multiple board sizes and complexity levels.

### **Summary of Findings**

* **GPT-4:** Only model that gave simulation-style reasoning and practical parameters.
* **Claude:** Most advanced theoretical analysis; strongest predictions and search-algorithm insight.
* **Gemini:** Good conceptual overviews but limited depth.

### **Overall Observation**

* **Small boards:** Greedy performs well.
* **Larger boards:** MCTS outperforms Alpha-Beta.
* **Claude** shines in theoretical analysis.
* **GPT-4** shines in experiment-oriented reasoning.
